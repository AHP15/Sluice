## D-032: Status colours are reserved, never carried by the accent or by colour alone

- **Status**: accepted
- **Date**: 2026-11-06
- **Date superseded**:
- **Date deprecated**:
- **Date promoted**:
- **Context**: Sluice's guardrails only work if they are noticed: production
  confirmation (D-027), the TLS opt-out warning (D-007), the read-only
  guarantee (T-061), open-transaction and lost-session banners (D-028), and
  truncation and budget notices (D-010). D-031 introduces a saturated cyan
  accent. An accent that also signals status, or a status shown only as a
  hue, would let a striking look blur exactly the signals that keep users out
  of trouble. That premise was implicit in the design and never stated.
- **Decision**: Colour roles are fixed and outlive any restyle.
  The accent (cyan) means **interaction only**: focus, selection, the primary
  action, and the user's own running query. It never marks a state.
  Four status hues are **reserved** and never used decoratively:
  - **red** (`#F26B68` dark / `#B42C29` light): destructive actions, errors,
    and production sessions (shown as a persistent red top rule);
  - **amber** (`#F5B841` / `#8A5300`): caution, such as the TLS opt-out, an
    open transaction, truncation, a budget warning, or an unverified flavour;
  - **green** (`#3DD68C` / `#0F7550`): a verified guarantee, such as
    read-only enforced by the database, or committed;
  - **violet** (`#A78BFA` / `#5B3FC4`): informational notes and navigator
    notices.
  Every status also carries an icon and a text label, so meaning never
  depends on colour alone.
- **Rationale**: Reserving hues makes a red rule or an amber banner
  unambiguous at a glance. That is the property the guardrail decisions
  assume and no styling choice should be able to erode. Keeping cyan off
  status leaves the accent free to be loud without being misread.
  Icon-plus-label covers colour-blind users and carries meaning across both
  themes, whose hues differ in lightness. This is recorded separately from
  D-031 so a future visual direction can replace D-031 without touching
  these roles.
