## D-039: SRV TXT options: accept replicaSet and authSource, refuse loadBalanced

- **Status**: accepted
- **Date**: 2026-11-07
- **Date superseded**:
- **Date deprecated**:
- **Date promoted**:
- **Context**: Resolving a `mongodb+srv` name returns member hosts, which the
  Mongo spec and the `mongodb` crate require to share the SRV name's parent
  domain, and an optional TXT record that may set only `authSource`,
  `replicaSet` and `loadBalanced`. Atlas relies on the TXT record to supply
  `authSource=admin`. Load-balanced mode is a different topology that T-046's
  `affinity: session` verdict and D-035's accounting were not validated
  against.
- **Decision**: Accept `replicaSet` and `authSource` from a TXT record at
  lower precedence than explicit profile or URL options. Refuse
  `loadBalanced=true` by name, whether it comes from TXT or a URL, until that
  topology is validated. The connection details show the resolved hosts and
  which options came from DNS.
- **Rationale**: Ignoring TXT reintroduces the Atlas authentication failure
  this work exists to fix, while the parent-domain rule already bounds where
  DNS can redirect and TXT cannot carry credentials or TLS settings. Refusing
  load-balanced mode costs little now — supporting it later is an added
  variant — and avoids declaring session behaviour no test has confirmed.
