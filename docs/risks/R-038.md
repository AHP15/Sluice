## R-038: One shared budget lets pinned editor sessions starve browse traffic to zero

- **Severity**: medium
- **Likelihood**: medium
- **Disposition**: mitigate
- **Date**: 2026-07-31
- **Description**: Folding pooled and pinned connections into a single ~5 ceiling removes the contradiction between them but also removes any floor under browse. Five editor tabs holding transactions -- which cannot be LRU-released by the decision's own rule -- consume the entire budget, so the navigator stops expanding and the grid stops loading pages while the user is mid-work. The separate budgets in D-015 were internally inconsistent but did guarantee browse some capacity; the unified budget is consistent and can reach zero.
- **Disposition rationale**: Medium severity at medium likelihood on its own, but it is the pressure valve for R-037's eligibility rule — tightening what may be evicted makes refusal the common path, and a refusal that stops the grid is worse than one that stops a new tab.
- **Action**: Guard: reserve a browse floor inside the single budget — pinned sessions may consume at most `budget - 2`, so the pool always retains at least two connections and the navigator and grid keep working regardless of editor tab state. Surface budget pressure directly: a connection-usage indicator listing which tabs hold sessions and which hold transactions, so the user can see why a new tab was refused and which tab to close. Allow the per-profile ceiling to be raised explicitly with a warning about `max_connections` citizenship, so the answer to sustained pressure is a deliberate choice rather than a hard wall. Mitigating tasks: T-044.
- **Related decisions**: D-028
