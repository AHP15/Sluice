## R-013: Pinned editor sessions exceed the deliberately small pool budget as tabs accumulate

- **Severity**: medium
- **Likelihood**: high
- **Disposition**: fix
- **Date**: 2026-07-31
- **Description**: The design caps the pool at roughly five connections per profile to avoid monopolising `max_connections`, and separately gives each SQL editor tab its own pinned connection held for the tab's lifetime. Six open editor tabs plus browse traffic already exceeds the intended budget, and idle pinned sessions hold server resources indefinitely — the specific behaviour cited as the reason to reject one-connection-per-tab. The two halves of the decision contradict each other under normal use.
- **Disposition rationale**: Medium severity at high likelihood, and it is an internal contradiction rather than an external constraint — six editor tabs already break the budget the same decision set, under ordinary use. A guard cannot reconcile two halves of one decision that disagree.
- **Action**: Superseded by D-028 — one per-profile ceiling now covers pooled and pinned connections together, with LRU release of idle pinned sessions (rolled back first, tab marked detached and reconnecting on next use), an idle timeout on pinned sessions, and refusal rather than eviction for a tab holding an open transaction. D-015 -> superseded.
- **Related decisions**: D-015
