## R-056: The loopback-seed refusal rejects standard local Docker replica sets that are not tunnels

- **Severity**: low
- **Likelihood**: high
- **Disposition**: mitigate
- **Date**: 2026-11-07
- **Date resolved**:
- **Description**: The usual local replica-set setup is a docker-compose cluster whose members advertise their service names (`mongo1:27017`, `mongo2:27017`), reached from the host through published ports and `/etc/hosts` entries mapping those names to `127.0.0.1`. The seed is `localhost:27017` and discovery yields members outside the configured seeds, so D-040 refuses it as a tunnel bypass even though every member resolves to loopback. Developers testing transactions locally — the case replica sets exist for — hit a refusal whose explanation (tunnels) does not describe their setup.
- **Disposition rationale**: Low severity at high likelihood; D-040's refusal is correct for tunnels, and this setup has a workaround inside the decision — list the advertised names as seeds, so no discovered member falls outside them.
- **Action**: Guard: the refusal message recognises the case where every discovered member's hostname resolves to loopback and says so, offering the exact `hosts = ["mongo1:27017", ...]` profile built from the advertised member names (which places every member inside the configured seeds) or a `Host` profile to the primary. PROFILE-FILE.md documents the docker-compose replica-set recipe. Mitigating tasks: T-107.
- **Related decisions**: D-040
