## R-044: Replica-set discovery connects around an `ssh -L` tunnel to the members' real hostnames

- **Severity**: high
- **Likelihood**: medium
- **Disposition**: fix
- **Date**: 2026-11-07
- **Date resolved**:
- **Description**: D-008 documents bring-your-own `ssh -L` with a `direct` profile as the supported way to reach a private database. Once D-033's `replica_set` (or a D-034 seed list / SRV) turns on topology discovery, the driver replaces the configured `localhost:<port>` seed with the hostnames the replica-set config advertises (`mongo-1.internal:27017`, ...) and connects to those directly — so it either fails to reach them or, where they are routable, opens connections outside the tunnel the user believes carries everything. Today's single-host `directConnection` behaviour is what makes the documented tunnel work, and the decisions do not say what discovery does to it.
- **Disposition rationale**: High severity at medium likelihood, and the decision's own text licensed the failure — a single host could trigger discovery — so the record had to change rather than gain a guard.
- **Action**: Superseded by D-040
- **Related decisions**: D-033, D-034, D-008
