## R-051: No setting carries a custom CA bundle or client certificate, so common managed databases cannot verify TLS

- **Severity**: medium
- **Likelihood**: high
- **Disposition**: fix
- **Date**: 2026-11-07
- **Date resolved**:
- **Description**: D-033 keeps TLS to the existing `tls` mode key and D-037 refuses parameters outside its lists, but neither gives a home to a trust anchor or client identity: Mongo `tlsCAFile` / `tlsCertificateKeyFile`, libpq `sslrootcert` / `sslcert` / `sslkey`. Amazon RDS and DocumentDB certificates chain to Amazon's own CAs, which are not in OS trust stores, and their documented URLs include exactly these parameters — so under `Verify` (the default) the handshake fails, and the only way to connect is `accept_invalid_certs`, the weakening D-007 tries to make exceptional.
- **Disposition rationale**: Medium severity at high likelihood: without a trust anchor, managed-database users are pushed to `accept_invalid_certs`, and D-033 forbade the only place it could live.
- **Action**: Superseded by D-042
- **Related decisions**: D-033, D-037, D-007
