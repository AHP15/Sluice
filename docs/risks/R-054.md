## R-054: SRV lookups through the crate's own resolver can ignore OS split-DNS that VPN users depend on

- **Severity**: medium
- **Likelihood**: medium
- **Disposition**: mitigate
- **Date**: 2026-11-07
- **Date resolved**:
- **Description**: The `mongodb` crate resolves SRV and TXT records itself (via `hickory-resolver`) instead of calling the OS resolver. On macOS, per-domain resolvers that corporate VPNs install (`/etc/resolver/*`, scoped `scutil` resolvers) are not reflected in `/etc/resolv.conf`, and on Windows NRPT rules are similarly invisible to a resolver reading interface configuration. A `mongodb+srv` name for a private cluster can then fail to resolve in Sluice while `mongosh` and the OS resolve it, and the error surfaces as a DNS failure with no hint that the cause is the resolver path.
- **Disposition rationale**: Medium severity at medium likelihood and environment-specific; replacing the crate's resolver is disproportionate, but the failure must be explained and the workaround obvious.
- **Action**: Guard: an SRV or TXT resolution failure maps to a D-017 hint naming the in-process resolver and suggesting a seed-list (`hosts = [..]`) profile; Mongo release verification includes resolving a private `mongodb+srv` name over a macOS VPN with split DNS, with the result recorded. Mitigating tasks: T-112.
- **Related decisions**: D-034, D-039
